## Methodology & limits
- Every claim carries a named benchmark, a number, a date, and a primary URL opened during this refresh (2026-11-01). Headline numbers were re-fetched and read off the page by the editor, not filled from memory.
- Self-reported numbers are labelled; third-party / reproducible evals (Artificial Analysis runs its own) are preferred. Vendor self-reports run materially above standardised harnesses — never cross-compare the two.
- **The Intelligence Index rebased v4.3.2 → v5.0 this refresh — the dominant event.** AA's methodology page (opened this session) states v5.0 "incorporates 9 evaluations" across four equally-weighted (25%) categories — **Knowledge Work** (AA-Briefcase v1.1, AutomationBench-AA, GDP.pdf), **Coding** (FrontierCode 2.0, Terminal-Bench 4.0), **Science** (CritPt v2 Hard Subset, Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1), **General** (AA-Omniscience v1.1, HLE-Diamond) — and that **GDPval-AA "was removed from the Intelligence Index in v5.0."** The scale recomposed and compressed. **Every open-weight and cheapest-capable Index number in this file is a v5.0 number and is NOT comparable to last week's v4.3.2 numbers** (GPT-6 Luna 38→28, MiMo-V2.6-Pro 46→30 across the rebase). Trust the **rank order** week-over-week; do not compare an Index *number* across versions.
- **Movement this week.** (1) **Open-weight reshuffle under v5.0:** DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (32) and GLM-5.3 (32) now co-lead; MiMo-V2.6-Pro slipped to #3 (30); three new open models entered the board — Ling 3.1 Flash 1019 (29, MIT, InclusionAI), MiniMax-M3.1-Flash (29, bespoke), Mistral Large 4 (26, bespoke).
