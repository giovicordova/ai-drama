## Telegram — Run 07:00

Artificial Analysis overhauled its Intelligence Index, the independent score it publishes to rank AI models. Version 5 puts 60% of the score on private or held-out test sets a model can't train on.

It drops three benchmarks and adds two: Cognition's private coding test FrontierCode 2.0, and Terminal-Bench-Science — 70 science tasks an agent runs in a terminal. A companion Progress Index v1 ships too.

Harder to game is the right direction; a score you can't study your way to means more.
