Independent Artificial Analysis now rates Google's Gemini 4 Argon level with OpenAI's flagship GPT-6 Astra — both score 53 on its Intelligence Index, a composite quality score.

The twist is cost. At Google's current 50% discount, Argon runs $1.99 to work through the benchmark versus Astra's $3.26 — the same score for 60% of the cost.

"Google returns as one of the top three labs on intelligence"

This morning it looked like one more model crowding the top; the cost gap is the sharper story.
