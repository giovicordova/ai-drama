- Self-reported numbers are labelled; third-party / reproducible evals (Artificial Analysis runs its own) are preferred. Vendor self-reports run materially above standardised harnesses — never cross-compare the two.
- **No index rebase this week.** The Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index reads **v5.0.1** on every model page this session, so this week's Index numbers are directly comparable to last week's.
- **Movement this week.** (1) **GPT-6.2 Astra arrives (released 17 Nov, proprietary):** debuts as AA's overall Intelligence Index #2 at 62 (behind Claude Fable 5.2 (Max) 65; ahead of Gemini 4 Neon (Xhigh) 61, Gemini 4 Carbon (High) 60, Opus 5.5 (Max) 59), and debuts tied for **multimodal #1 on MMMU-Pro at 89%** with Gemini 4 Carbon (Medium) — last week Gemini 4 Carbon was the lone named #1. (3) **Fastest still dark** (third refresh running): AA's per-provider output-speed leaderboard shows no readable tokens/sec this session, so the row stays dropped. Coding (GPT-5.6 Sol, 65.9%), reasoning (Fable 5.2, 74.3%), long-context (Opus 4.6, 76%), agentic and cheapest-capable leaders all held on their own metrics.
