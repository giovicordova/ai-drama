## Methodology & limits
- Every claim carries a named benchmark, a number, a date, and a primary URL opened during this refresh (2026-11-15). Headline numbers were re-fetched and read off the page by the editor, not filled from memory.
- Self-reported numbers are labelled; third-party / reproducible evals (Artificial Analysis runs its own) are preferred. Vendor self-reports run materially above standardised harnesses — never cross-compare the two.
- **No index rebase this week.** The Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index reads **v5.0.1** on every model page this session, so this week's Index numbers are directly comparable to last week's.
- **Movement this week.** (1) **New overall + reasoning #1:** Claude Fable 5.2 (released 9 Nov) leads AA's overall Intelligence Index at 65 (Gemini 4 Carbon (High) 60, Opus 5.5 (Max) 59) and tops Humanity's Last Exam at 74.3% (Opus 5.5's 61.4% out of the named top-3). (2) **New open-weight #4:** Inkling 2 Small (Apache 2.0, Thinking Machines) debuts at Index 31; MiniMax-M3.1-Flash (was 29) and Muse Spark Open (was 25) dropped off the board; Kimi K3.1 Pro holds #1 at 46. (3) **New multimodal #2:** Gemini 4 Argon (Medium) debuts tied with Opus 5.5 at MMMU-Pro 88%, behind Gemini 4 Carbon (Medium) 89%. (4) **Fastest still dark:** AA's per-provider output-speed leaderboard shows no readable tokens/sec this session (Performance Benchmarking v3.0.0), so the row stays dropped. Coding, long-context, agentic and cheapest-capable leaders held on their own (non-Index) metrics.
- **Open-weight licence watch — the #1 is NOT permissive.** The open leader, **Kimi K3.1 Pro (46)**, ships under a bespoke "Kimi K3.1-Pro Licence." The highest-Index **permissively-licensed** open models are **DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (33, MIT)**, then **Inkling 2 Small (31, Apache 2.0)**, with **MiMo-V2.6-Pro (30, MIT)** and **Ling 3.1 Flash 1019 (30, MIT)** also permissive. GLM-5.3 (32) ships under a bespoke licence. Rows rank on the independent AA Index but flag licence and provenance — read the licence before deploying.
- **Saturation watch:** GPQA Diamond (~94% top cluster) and τ²-Bench Telecom (~99%) have largely saturated; AIME 2025/2026 are at/near a perfect score for top models. Where a flagship bench has saturated, this file leads with a still-discriminating eval (HLE for reasoning, Terminal-Bench Hard for agents).
- **Stated context window ≠ usable context.** The long-context row cites a multi-needle retrieval eval at depth, not a spec-sheet number; advertised 1M (and larger) windows degrade sharply before their stated limit (Gemini 3.1 Pro: 84.9% at 128k → 26.3% at 1M).
